As defined by this repository's `GRACEY_DESIGN_REFERENCE_SYSTEM.md`, preserve the three distinct levels:

1. **Reference**: an externally or internally sourced graphic object and its actual provenance.
2. **Extracted design principle**: reusable relationship in that work, with stated confidence and limits.

No external creative binary is mirrored, copied, relicensed or imported into the F&F product.

## Cross-channel application and governance

**Web:** Site v5 preview (hero/footer dot component, editorial glyphs, purpose-first copy).  
**Documents/whitepapers:** source-bound editorial title hierarchy, own authored artwork, F1–F3 provenance.  
**Presentations:** reusable intro, case, diagram and evidence states, not one decorative effect on every slide.  
**Miro:** primary runtime for causal/actor/flow thinking; semantic source remains external.  
**Figma/Canva:** downstream visual production, not independent claim authorisation.  
**Social:** recognisable and light editorial signature; public release requires attribution and rights.

**Invalid design transfers:** copying a benchmark firm's trademark colours/corporate diagram; using noncanonical generic green palette instead of supplied F&F teal/orange; treating a PDF cover as rights to reproduce an unrelated paper; treating the site's visual version and corporate logo version as the same number; replacing real sources with generated images or synthetic project data.

## Decision and lifecycle

**Reference status:** `reviewed` for original F&F logo and actual holding-site dots; `reviewed`/qualitative for public benchmark sources.  
**Principle status:** `principle-extracted` with explicit caveats.  
**Application status:** `applied` to **private Site v5 prototype** and **candidate company profile**, not to approved final public corporate use.

Further release requires original master/derivative verification, Director approval of full profile, licensed typography/print controls and public Site S09/S10 gates.
